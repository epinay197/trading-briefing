The two layers
Map (fixed all day): MenthorQ EOD levels, built from settled open interest at yesterday's close. Open interest cannot change during the session — these strikes do not move. It tells you how price reacts when it gets there. Blind spot: today's 0DTE volume creates positioning that does not reach open interest until tomorrow.
