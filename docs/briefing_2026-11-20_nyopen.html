Session Playbook — how to trade the levels above
The two layers
Map (fixed all day): MenthorQ EOD levels, built from settled open interest at yesterday's close. Open interest cannot change during the session — these strikes do not move. They tell you where price reacts. Weather (moves constantly): live gamma on that same open interest. Gamma reprices with spot, time and IV, so intensity swings hard even though no strike moved. It tells you how price reacts when it gets there. Blind spot: today's 0DTE volume creates positioning that does not reach open interest until tomorrow. Your only proxies are the gamma sign and the put/call volume ratio.
