The two layers
Map (fixed all day): MenthorQ EOD levels, built from settled open interest at yesterday's close. Open interest cannot change during the session — these strikes do not move. They tell you where price reacts. Weather (moves constantly): live gamma on that same open interest. Gamma reprices with spot, time and IV, so intensity swings hard even though no strike moved.
